Highlands & Balds

Six days across the highest meadows in the Blue Ridge — wild ponies at Grayson Highlands, a lazy canoe down the New River, waterfall swimming holes, and a night camped on top of the Roan.

  1. Fri Aug 7 drive · airbnb

    Arlington → creekside cabin near Hillsville, VA

    Roll out around 2:30pm, ~4¾ hr down I-81 and I-77. A creekside cabin with a deck for stargazing and a fire pit. Dinner on the road or takeout, then creek sounds and an early night.

  2. Sat Aug 8 backpack · camp

    Grayson Highlands → Mount Rogers high country

    Slow morning — checkout 11am, and Massie Gap is only ~1 hr 20 min away through Galax and Volney. If we're up early: breakfast in Galax and a leg-stretcher on the New River Trail rail-trail along Chestnut Creek (leashed dogs welcome). Then to Massie Gap (parking check-in 1pm). Hike ~4 mi up the Rhododendron Trail and along the AT over Wilburn Ridge — this is pony country, so Poppy stays on leash and pretends to be calm. Camp near Rhododendron Gap / Thomas Knob, dinner on the stove, sunset from the rocks around 8:20pm.

    Campfires are banned in the high country this year — stove only. Bear boxes at camp for food, trash, toothpaste, and Poppy's kibble.

  3. Sun Aug 9 hike out · airbnb

    Morning ponies, then down to West Jefferson, NC

    Coffee at sunrise, ponies in the morning light, and back at the car by noon (parking checkout). Drive ~1 hr to our cottage in West Jefferson — a 5 min walk to downtown, with a hot tub and fire pit. Hot showers, laundry, then an evening stroll: downtown galleries and Ashe County Cheese, or the short summit trail at Mount Jefferson (leashed pups welcome) for a sunset overlook.

  4. Mon Aug 10 canoe · airbnb

    Canoe the South Fork of the New River

    RiverGirl Fishing Co. in Todd (~15 min away) puts all three of us in a canoe — dogs officially welcome. The South Fork through Todd is slow and glassy Class I, more drifting than paddling. Afternoon options: Rough Ridge on the Parkway (1.5 mi round trip, boardwalk ledges, huge Grandfather Mountain views) or the flat, shaded Bass Lake carriage trails in Blowing Rock — both leash-friendly. Then hot tub. Same beds as last night.

  5. Tue Aug 11 swim · backpack · camp

    Elk River Falls, then camp on Grassy Ridge Bald

    On the way to the Roan: Elk River Falls, ~10 min off the route near Elk Park — a half-mile walk to a 50-ft waterfall pouring into a big emerald pool. Swim from the bank (no jumping — people get hurt there), lunch on the rocks, Poppy leashed back from the lip. Then on to Carvers Gap and just 2.5 mi of trail over Round and Jane Balds to camp at 6,100 ft on Grassy Ridge — 360° of mountains, sunset around 8:15pm, and more stars than either of us has seen all year.

    Camp plan: past the summit outcrops the trail drops ~0.4 mi southeast into a small saddle — established sites in the clearing and the rhododendron pockets, sheltered from the west wind, with the outcrops a two-minute walk back up for sunset. Two trickle springs nearby (one ~100 yd down a worn side trail on the NE side, one just below the saddle) — filter them, and still carry ~2L each up plus Poppy's. Established bare-dirt sites only — the ridge grass hides rare plants (Gray's lily grows here). No bear boxes; food gets hung or canistered. Big wind → tuck fully into the rhododendron; storms → Stan Murray Shelter, ~1.4 mi up the AT, is the fallback camp.

  6. Wed Aug 12 hike out · swim · drive

    Sunrise on the ridge, then home — maybe via the Cascades

    Sunrise at ~6:35am is the show — then break camp, hike out, and point the car north. Straight home is ~6¾ hr. Or the nicer version: ~3 hr to Cascades Falls in Pembroke — a 4-mile creekside walk to a 66-ft waterfall with a big swimming hole (leashed dogs fine, $3/car) to rinse off the backpacking grime — then a short hop to Roanoke for a celebration dinner and a night's stop.

  7. Thu Aug 13 buffer

    Home

    Built-in slack day — either already home and doing laundry, or an easy final 3½ hr from Roanoke.

Packing notes

Mountain weather

  • Days in the 70s, nights in the 50s at elevation — real sleeping bags, warm layer each
  • Afternoon thunderstorms are routine; the balds are totally exposed, so we summit mornings and evenings
  • Rain shells even if the forecast looks clean

Poppy's kit

  • 6-ft leash required everywhere (and the ponies will test it)
  • Her food counts as bear food — stored with ours, never in the tent
  • Sleeping pad or blanket for cold nights, water bowl for the ridge
  • Towel — between the canoe and the waterfalls, she will be wet most of this trip

Swimming holes

  • Water shoes for creek bottoms; the water runs 60s°F even in August
  • Swim from the bank at Elk River Falls — no jumping
  • Quick-dry towels + dry bag for phones

Camp rules this year

  • No campfires in either high-country area — stove with an on/off valve only
  • All smellables (food, trash, toiletries) in bear boxes at Grayson, hung or canistered on the Roan
  • Zip the packs — Grayson's ponies chew salty straps for sport
